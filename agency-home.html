★ The Candidate Side

Why 14,000+ Texans chose this platform to start their career

Your next hire is already here. Understanding what candidates experience — and why they stay engaged — is what makes your pipeline stronger.

One profile, every Texas agency

Candidates build a single profile — TCOLE license type, experience level, region preferences — and it's instantly visible to every participating department. No re-applying from scratch for each opening.

Built exclusively for Texas law enforcement

Every listing is from a verified TCOLE-licensed agency. Candidates aren't sifting through unrelated postings — they come to this platform specifically to pursue a career in Texas policing.

Guided by regional Ambassadors

Each of the 13 Texas regions has a sworn peace officer who mentors candidates through the process — answering questions about academy life, hiring standards, and what serving in their area is really like.

★ Eligibility

Any TCOLE-licensed Texas agency can participate

If your department employs or is authorized to employ peace officers, jailers, or telecommunicators under TCOLE licensing — you qualify. No minimum size, no minimum postings required.

  • Municipal police departments
  • County sheriff's offices & constable precincts
  • State agencies (DPS, Parks & Wildlife, etc.)
  • University & campus police
  • Hospital districts & transit authorities
  • Independent School District police
