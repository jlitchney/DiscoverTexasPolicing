San Antonio Police Department patrol officers are responsible for responding to calls for service, conducting investigations, and building community relationships across the city's diverse neighborhoods. Officers may be assigned to specialized units including K-9, Motors, Community Action Team, and SWAT based on department needs and personal qualifications.
  • Must be 21 years of age or older at time of hire
  • U.S. citizen or permanent resident eligible for citizenship
  • Valid Texas driver's license (or ability to obtain within 90 days)
  • High school diploma or GED equivalent
  • No felony convictions or disqualifying criminal history
  • Lateral applicants: Current or previous TCOLE-licensed peace officer with 1+ year of sworn experience
  • Starting salary: $58,500 (Entry) · $66,000–$78,000 (Lateral, DOE)
  • Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance
  • Texas Municipal Retirement System (TMRS) pension
  • Paid academy and field training for entry-level candidates
  • Uniform and equipment allowance
  • Lateral hire signing bonus up to $10,000
