About this role

The Austin Police Department is hiring sworn officers for assignment across Patrol, Region Specialty Units, and Investigations. We accept both Lateral applicants (1+ year sworn experience in any state) and Entry applicants (no prior license required — paid academy provided).

As a Police Officer with APD you will respond to calls for service, conduct preliminary investigations, complete reports, and serve the residents of Austin with professionalism and respect for civil rights.

Minimum qualifications

  • U.S. citizen, age 21+ at appointment
  • High school diploma or GED (60+ college credits preferred)
  • Valid Texas driver's license at appointment
  • Vision correctable to 20/20; pass POPAT physical assessment
  • No felony convictions, no Class A misdemeanors involving moral turpitude
  • Honorable military discharge (if applicable)
  • Pass psychological, polygraph, drug screen, medical, and background investigation

Compensation & benefits

  • Starting pay: $74,800 (Entry) / $86,400+ (Lateral, depending on years)
  • Sign-on bonus of $15,000 (paid in three installments)
  • Bilingual stipend up to $325/month
  • Education incentives (Associate $100/mo, Bachelor $200/mo, Master $300/mo)
  • 20-year retirement (TMRS Group 4) + 457(b)
  • Medical/Dental/Vision for employee + dependents at low cost
  • Take-home vehicle for assigned officers within 30 miles
