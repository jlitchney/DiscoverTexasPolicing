Pre-screening — High-profile disqualifiers

Texas peace officer, jailer and telecommunicator standards include statutory disqualifiers under Texas Occupations Code §1701. Answer honestly — your responses are encrypted and only become visible to an agency after you apply to them.

ⓘ

What "high-profile disqualifier" means

These three categories — felony convictions, dishonorable military discharge, and history of drug abuse — disqualify candidates statewide under TCOLE rules. Answering Yes means we won't waste your time submitting applications to agencies that cannot consider you. Most agencies offer alternative pathways (telecommunicator, civilian, support roles) you can still pursue.
