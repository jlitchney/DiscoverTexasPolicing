Step 5

Pre-screen rules

Pre-screen rules automatically flag or disqualify candidates who don't meet your criteria — saving your team hours of manual review.

Automatically disqualifies candidates who: have been convicted of a felony; received a dishonorable military discharge; have substantiated drug abuse history within 10 years; or have had a peace officer license revoked by TCOLE. These rules align with Texas Occupations Code Chapter 1701.

Custom rules are reviewed by TPCA staff within 1 business day before they go live.

Disqualified candidates receive a respectful email explaining the reason and linking to TCOLE's expunction and waiver resources where applicable. Reduces support inquiries to your agency.
