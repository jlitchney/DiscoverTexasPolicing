Why 13 regions?

Aligned with Texas Comptroller standards

DiscoverTexasPolicing uses the 13 Uniform State Service Regions of Texas — the same boundaries adopted across state agencies — for consistency with regional reporting and resource allocation.

Cross-region hiring

Apply anywhere in Texas with one profile

Set your geographic preferences across multiple regions. Your single statewide profile is shared with hiring agencies anywhere in Texas you mark as eligible.

Local partnerships

Recruitment Ambassadors in every region

Each of the 13 regions has at least one volunteer Ambassador — a sworn Texas peace officer who can answer questions about hiring standards, academy life, or careers in your area.
