NOTES
================================================================
- Filenames must match exactly (case-sensitive on most servers).
- JPG, PNG, or WEBP all work — just keep the .jpg extension shown
  above (or change extension references in the HTML).
- Recommended sizes:
    Hero backgrounds:   2000–2600 px wide, landscape
    Specialty tiles:    1200–1600 px wide, 4:3 ratio works best
    Banners:            1600 px wide, 16:9 ratio
    Portraits:          600 px square, face centered
- If you'd rather not use a photo in some slot, just leave the
  filename out — the page falls back to a clean navy gradient.
