ALL-STAR RECRUITER

The full recruiting CRM built for law enforcement agencies.

DiscoverTexasPolicing gives your agency access to the statewide candidate pool. All-Star Recruiter gives you the tools to engage, nurture, and hire them — with SMS campaigns, automated workflows, AI chatbots, and more.

Why agencies upgrade

One platform. Every stage of recruiting.

From the first text message to a signed offer letter — All-Star Recruiter keeps every candidate, communication, and document in one place.

SMS & Automated Outreach

Send bulk SMS campaigns, schedule automated follow-up sequences, and trigger messages based on candidate actions — all without manual effort. Each plan includes a monthly text allowance.

Workflows & Automations

Build drag-and-drop hiring workflows that automatically move candidates through your pipeline, assign tasks to recruiters, send notifications, and request documents — no manual chasing required.

AI Chatbot & Engagement

Deploy an AI chatbot on your agency's recruitment page to answer candidate questions 24/7, qualify prospects, collect contact info, and book calls — without tying up your recruiting staff.

Events & Hiring Campaigns

Promote open houses, testing days, and career fairs with the built-in events system. Run targeted advertising campaigns across social channels to reach candidates who aren't actively browsing job boards.

Mobile App & File Requests

Recruiters manage their pipeline from a native mobile app. Send candidates secure file request links to collect background forms, DD-214s, transcripts, and certifications — no email attachments needed.

White Label & Custom Pages

Publish custom recruitment webpages under your agency's brand. Manage tracking links for job fairs, social posts, and partner referrals — so you always know which channel is producing your best candidates.
